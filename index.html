My Profile & Journey

From curiosity about how systems communicate to building enterprise infrastructure and competing at the national level.

From Curiosity to Cloud Infrastructure

I started my journey in computer networking with a deep curiosity about how systems communicate. I am the type of person who isn't satisfied with just theory—I love building real projects, troubleshooting complex issues, and designing infrastructure from scratch.

Through continuous learning and competition, I developed strong skills in Linux Server, Network Infrastructure, Virtualization, and Cloud Technology. I actively manage school networks and servers as a core member of Team Computer SMKN 1 Liwa.

My focus is on becoming a Professional Cloud Engineer or Network Engineer. I thrive in environments where I can design enterprise networks, deploy scalable server architectures, and solve critical system problems.

I don't just learn technology; I implement it. Breaking things and fixing them is part of my process. Whether it's configuring VLANs on Mikrotik, deploying Docker containers, or setting up a secure Cloudflare Tunnel, I ensure I understand the underlying protocol, not just the UI.

Work Philosophy

I believe that the best way to learn technology is through hands-on implementation. Every failure is a debug log, and every success is a deployed service. I am highly adaptable, eager to learn new stacks, and obsessed with system optimization and security.

Daily Routine

My typical day involves checking server monitoring dashboards (Uptime Kuma, Netdata), reviewing firewall logs, experimenting with new Docker containers, and studying for advanced cloud certifications. I also dedicate time to help my school's IT team ensure the network runs smoothly for practical exams.

Road to LKS & OJM

The journey to the national stage was intense. Hundreds of students competed, but only the best continued. Here is a breakdown of my preparation and the challenges I overcame.

The Challenge

Competing in ITNSA requires mastery of enterprise-level networking, server deployment, and security implementation under strict time limits. It's not just about knowing commands; it's about designing resilient systems.

  • Enterprise Network Design

    Multi-zone topology, DMZ, VLAN segmentation, OSPF/BGP routing.

  • Server Deployment & Automation

    Debian servers, HAProxy, Keepalived, automated backups.

  • Network Security & Firewall

    Mikrotik Firewall, VPN tunnels, IPSec, SSL inspection.

  • Infrastructure Troubleshooting

    Packet sniffing, log analysis, disaster recovery simulation.

My Preparation

I spent months simulating real-world scenarios in my home lab. If something broke, I didn't reset it—I fixed it. I replicated past national-level modules and timed myself to ensure I could deliver under pressure.

  • Advanced Linux Administration: Scripting, cron jobs, service management, user privileges.
  • Mikrotik Enterprise Routing: Static & dynamic routing, bridge, VLAN, QoS, queue trees.
  • Server High Availability: HAProxy load balancing, Keepalived failover, database replication.
  • VPN & Secure Tunneling: Site-to-Site VPN, Client-to-Site, WireGuard, OpenVPN.
  • Disaster Recovery: rsync, cron automated backups, Proxmox snapshots.
